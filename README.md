# Verificación oficial de mamá 💜/ Mom's Birthday Website

A funny birthday website I made for my mom. She has to verify that she's really my mom, confirm I'm the favorite child and then she gets a cake and a message. 

Play it here: https://marthahere.github.io/mom-birthday/

## What's in it

Identity check: "Are you my mom?" Answering no denies access, but she can try again.
The favorite child question: A rigged question. The "No" button runs away and gets smaller while "Yes" keeps growing until "No" disappears completely.
The date question: Asks whether it's her birthday today. Either answer leads to a birthday message because she deserves to be celebrated every day.
The cake: Blow out the candles to trigger a confetti animation.
The message: A short, heartfelt note to end the experience.

## Reusing it

To use it for another birthday, change the name, date and messages in the variables at the top of the script. The date question and messages pull the name and date from their respective variables, so you don't have to edit the text separately.
